# "Illegal to Be Lonely" (Switzerland's guinea pig law + the rent-a-guinea-pig service -> Feeloy)

Composition (to build once the take arrives): `PigsShort`, code `src/videos/pigs/`, output `out/pigs_final.mp4`.

## Facts (with sources)
- Since 2008 Swiss animal-welfare rules make it illegal to keep a single guinea pig: social species such as guinea pigs
  must have a companion of their own kind; the law reasons a human is no substitute for another guinea pig, however
  much the owner loves it or talks to it. Enforcement is gentle.
  - https://www.mentalfloss.com/animals/pets/its-illegal-own-single-guinea-pig-switzerland
  - https://theworld.org/stories/2011-09-21/rent-guinea-pig-switzerland
  - https://www.scandinaviastandard.com/b-since-2008-it-has-been-illegal-in-switzerland-to-own-a-single-guinea-pig-because-the-law-classes-them-as-social-animals-that-suffer-when-kept-alone-a-rule-that-created-a-genuine-business-a/
- Guinea pigs rarely die at the same time; when one of a pair dies, the owner would be breaking the law by keeping the
  survivor alone; buying a younger one locks the owner into an endless cycle.
  - https://theworld.org/stories/2013/08/14/rent-guinea-pig-switzerland
  - https://www.metafilter.com/107677/No-more-lonesome-rodents
- Priska Küng, a teacher by profession, in a village near Zürich, lives with about 80 guinea pigs and runs a
  rent-a-guinea-pig service: the survivor gets a companion that goes back to her after the owner's pet has died
  (deposit 50-60 francs; two to three enquiries a week).
  - https://begin-english.ru/stati-na-angliiskom/rent-a-guinea-pig-service-takes-off-in-switzerland (dpa report)
  - https://neatorama.com/2011/09/21/guinea-pig-rental-service
- Matchmaking: a young, temperamental guinea pig can annoy a four- or five-year-old one; but she has also known guinea
  pig grandpas to feel rejuvenated by a younger companion.
  - https://begin-english.ru/stati-na-angliiskom/rent-a-guinea-pig-service-takes-off-in-switzerland
- Depiction: Priska is a real person, drawn as a warm, generic guinea-pig lady; no real logos or names on screen beyond
  her first name in the narration. Deaths stay off screen ("when one goes", "when its time comes").

## Script (160 counted words)
1. In Switzerland, it is illegal to own just one guinea pig.
2. The reason? It might get lonely.
3. Since two thousand eight, Swiss law says social animals need a friend of their own kind.
4. You can love it and talk to it every day. Doesn't count.
5. But guinea pigs rarely pass away together. So when one goes, the owner is suddenly breaking the law.
6. Buy a young one, and the cycle never ends.
7. Enter Priska. A schoolteacher, who lives with eighty guinea pigs.
8. She runs a rent-a-guinea-pig service.
9. Your lonely pet gets a roommate. And when its time comes, the roommate goes home.
10. She even plays matchmaker. Sometimes a tired old grandpa gets a young friend, and feels young again.
11. A whole country made sure no guinea pig is ever alone.
12. Nobody wrote that law for people.
13. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny is always there, so you're never the only one.
14. Search Feeloy on the App Store.
