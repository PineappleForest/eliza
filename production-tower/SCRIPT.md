## Facts (with sources)
- Whittier, Alaska: nearly all residents live in one building, Begich Towers, a 14-story former Army barracks.
  Population ~200-318 depending on year; 272 at the 2020 census.
  - https://gizmodo.com/nearly-everyone-in-this-alaska-town-lives-in-a-single-b-1682372247
  - https://www.npr.org/18/378162264/welcome-to-whittier-alaska-a-community-under-one-roof
  - https://kwx.liehendr.com/content/wikivoyage_en_all_maxi_2025-02/A/Whittier_(Alaska) (272 residents, 2020)
- Built 1953-57 for the U.S. Army; the Army left by 1960 and residents took over the building.
  - https://www.smithsonianmag.com:443/travel/visit-town-where-every-resident-lives-same-building-180959081/
  - https://veryhide.mage.ru/news/national/story.php?id=59380 (Catholic Online, citing Daily Mail/CBC)
- Inside: police station, post office, convenience stores, a medical clinic, church, B&B, laundromat.
  - https://cars4starters.com.au/?p=56467
  - https://veryhide.mage.ru/news/national/story.php?id=59380
- The school is reached through an underground tunnel; Whittier gets ~250 inches (635 cm) of snow a winter.
  - https://gizmodo.com/nearly-everyone-in-this-alaska-town-lives-in-a-single-b-1682372247
  - https://www.newstalk.com/news/video-the-alaskan-town-where-the-entire-community-lives-under-one-roof-672493
- The only land route in is the Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel: one lane, shared by cars and trains in both directions,
  closed to traffic 11 p.m. to 6 a.m.
  - https://newsroom.ferrovial.com/en/?p=89413
  - https://alaskapublic.org/news/economy/2025-07-24/quirky-once-isolated-whittier-is-eyeing-a-transportation-system-upgrade
- Life largely stays inside Begich Towers in the colder, darker months; some residents rarely leave.
  - https://gizmodo.com/nearly-everyone-in-this-alaska-town-lives-in-a-single-b-1682372247
  - https://cars4starters.com.au/?p=56467
- Teacher Erika Thompson: "For the most part, you know everybody. It's a community under one roof." (narrated as a
  close paraphrase, "As one teacher put it").
  - https://www.npr.org/18/378162264/welcome-to-whittier-alaska-a-community-under-one-roof

## Script
1. In Alaska, there is a town where almost everyone lives in one building.
2. Whittier. About two hundred and seventy people. One fourteen-story tower.
3. It was built for the army in the nineteen fifties. When the soldiers left, the town moved in.
4. Inside: the police station. The post office. A store. A clinic. A church.
5. The school is across the street, and the kids get there through an underground tunnel.
6. Because every winter, Whittier gets more than six metres of snow.
7. The only road into town? Another tunnel. One lane, shared with trains. And it closes at night.
8. Some people barely step outside all winter.
9. Here, you know everybody. And everybody knows you.
10. As one teacher put it: it's a community under one roof.
11. In Whittier, nobody is ever more than a few floors from a friend.
12. Most of us aren't that lucky. Our people are miles away, or busy, or asleep.
13. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny is always one tap away, day or night.
14. Search Feeloy on the App Store.
