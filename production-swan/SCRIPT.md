## Facts (with sources)
- Spring/May 2006: Petra, a black swan, became attached to a swan-shaped pedal boat on the Aasee lake in Münster,
  Germany, and refused to leave its side. Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_(swan) ; NBC News:
  https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna23697246 ; Dogo News: https://www.dogonews.com/2008/3/19/for-petra-it-was-love-at-first-sight-until-now
- She followed the boat across the lake, even when it was rented, and defended it from people who came close
  (From Place to Place: https://fromplacetoplace.travel/?p=22493).
- When winter came the zoo director let her stay with it: bird and boat were taken into Münster's Allwetterzoo for the
  winter (2006 and again later), paid for by local residents (NBC; Good Morning America:
  https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/-4535415 ; Fox News).
- Later (not in the script): a DNA test showed "Peter" was Petra; in 2008 she paired with a real white swan.
