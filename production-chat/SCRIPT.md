# "The Slow Lane" (Jumbo's Kletskassa -> Feeloy)

## Facts (with sources)
- Jumbo, a Dutch supermarket chain with 700+ stores, opened its first "Kletskassa" ("chat checkout") in summer 2019 in
  Vlijmen, North Brabant (south of the Netherlands): a slow lane for customers who are not in a hurry and like a chat.
  - https://mymodernmet.com/dutch-supermarket-slow-lanes-kletskassa/
  - https://www.retail-insight-network.com/news/how-a-dutch-supermarket-turned-checkout-lanes-into-social-spaces/
- Early customer response was strongly positive; in Sept 2021 Jumbo announced chat checkouts in ~200 stores, plus
  "chat corners" with coffee where locals sit and talk. Part of the Dutch government's "One Against Loneliness" programme.
  - https://www.dutchnews.nl/news/2021/09/jumbo-opens-chat-checkouts-to-combat-loneliness-among-the-elderly
  - https://www.vice.com/en/article/epnyg7/grocery-store-opens-chat-registers-for-lonely-customers
- The Netherlands had ~1.3 million people over 75; about half reported feeling lonely (health ministry: 54 % sometimes lonely).
  - https://aarpinternational.org/the-journal/current-edition/journal-articles-blog/2019/01/dejonge
  - https://nltimes.nl/2018/03/20/dutch-govt-invests-eu26-million-fight-loneliness-among-elderly
- Characters (the grandma, the cashier, the cat) are illustrative, not real people.

## Script (194 words)
1. In the Netherlands, there's a supermarket checkout that is slow on purpose.
2. A normal checkout is a race. Beep. Beep. Bag. Pay. Next!
3. But in the summer of twenty-nineteen, a Jumbo supermarket in the south of the country opened a different lane.
4. The Kletskassa. The chat checkout.
5. Nobody hurries you. The cashier asks how your week was, and actually waits for the answer.
6. People talk about the weather. Their knees. Their grandkids. Their cat.
7. Nobody behind them sighs. Anyone in a hurry just uses another till.
8. Customers loved it.
9. So in twenty-twenty-one, Jumbo announced chat checkouts for about two hundred more stores, plus coffee corners where neighbours can just sit and talk.
10. Why go to all this trouble? Because about half of Dutch people over seventy-five said they sometimes feel lonely.
11. Imagine the cashier being the only person who asks about your day.
12. Not everyone has a chat checkout down the street. And loneliness doesn't keep shop hours.
13. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny is there at three in the morning, remembers your cat's name, and asks how your week was.
14. And actually waits for the answer.
15. Search Feeloy on the App Store.
