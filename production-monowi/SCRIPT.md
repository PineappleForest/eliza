# "Population: One" (Monowi, Nebraska -> Feeloy)

Composition (to build once the take arrives): `MonowiShort`, code `src/videos/monowi/`, output `out/monowi_final.mp4`.
Narration: ElevenLabs, voice "Mark". ~150 words (Mark reads ~2.75 words/s -> ~58-62 s).

## Facts (with sources)
- Monowi's peak was in the 1930s (population ~130). By the 2000 census only Rudy and Elsie Eiler lived there;
  Rudy died in 2004 and Elsie became the sole resident.
  - https://amusingplanet.com/2011/05/monowi-town-with-population-of-one.html
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monowi,_Nebraska
- How it emptied: grocery stores closed, schools were combined, people left.
  - https://medium.com/lessons-from-history/the-resilient-lone-resident-of-americas-smallest-incorporated-town-86315f510b35
- She is mayor, clerk, treasurer, secretary, librarian and runs the tavern; issues herself the annual liquor licence and
  signs it; every year hangs a sign in the tavern for the mayoral election and votes for herself.
  - https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2025/5/inside-the-town-with-lowest-population-where-one-woman-is-mayor-librarian-and-bartender
  - https://www.myjoyonline.com/the-town-with-a-population-of-one/
- Rudy's dying wish was a public library from his books: Rudy's Library, ~5,000 volumes, a key in the tavern, honour system.
  - https://www.myjoyonline.com/the-town-with-a-population-of-one/
- The tavern draws regular customers from as far as 80 miles away.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monowi,_Nebraska
- Elsie is a real, living person: drawn as a warm, generic grey-haired woman, never mocked. Rudy appears only as a
  photo frame and his books. No real logos.

## Script (~150 words)
1. There's a town in America with a population of one.
2. Her name is Elsie.
3. Monowi, Nebraska once had over a hundred people. Then the shops closed. The schools merged. People left.
4. By the year two thousand, only two were left: Elsie and her husband, Rudy.
5. In two thousand four, Rudy died.
6. So Elsie became the mayor. And the clerk. And the treasurer. And the librarian. And the bartender.
7. Every year, she issues herself a liquor licence... and signs it.
8. Every election, she votes for herself.
9. Rudy loved books. His last wish was a public library. So Elsie opened one: five thousand books, open to anyone.
10. And she kept the tavern open.
11. People drive up to eighty miles just to sit at her bar.
12. Population: one. But never alone.
13. Not everyone has a tavern full of friends down the road.
14. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny is always there, remembers your story, and keeps the door open, day and night.
15. Search Feeloy on the App Store.

## Shot plan (draft, timed to the take later)
1. Hook: a prairie road at golden hour; the town sign "MONOWI POP. 1" (number ticks 130 -> 1 later); push in.
2. Elsie waves from the tavern porch.
3. Time-lapse feel: a small street of houses whose windows go dark one by one; a shop shutter; a school bell silent.
4. Elsie and Rudy on the porch (two characters), old photo look.
5. An empty chair; a framed photo of Rudy on the bar.
6. Elsie swaps hats/badges per title (mayor sash, clerk visor, librarian glasses, bartender towel), cut on each word.
7. She stamps a licence, then signs it: a big stamp + signature insert.
8. A ballot box: one ballot drops in; a "WINNER" ribbon pops.
9. Rudy's Library: a little shed full of books, a key on a hook, she unlocks it.
10. The tavern sign lights up.
11. Cars arrive down the long road; strangers at the bar laughing with her (2-3 people).
12. The sign again, "POP. 1", with the tavern windows warm behind it.
13. A lonely person at night in a city flat, no tavern nearby.
14. Phone insert: Jenny, a door icon, a heart.
15. End card.
