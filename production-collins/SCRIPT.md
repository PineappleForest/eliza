## Facts (with sources)
- July 1969: Collins piloted the command module Columbia alone in lunar orbit while Armstrong and Aldrin landed.
  Astronomy: https://www.astronomy.com/space-exploration/michael-collins-apollo-11-command-module-pilot-dies-at-90/
- Radio contact with Earth cut off behind the Moon each orbit: "more than 45 minutes" (Deccan Herald), 47-48 minutes
  in other accounts (Astronomy quotes "this 47 minutes of each lunar revolution"; Pan Macmillan: 48). Script says
  "about forty-five minutes".
  https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/science/remembering-the-astronaut-who-carried-the-fire-982683.html
- Called "the loneliest man in history" (NASA's acting administrator in 2021 via Space Center Houston:
  https://spacecenter.org/?p=46939 ; Astronomy).
- Asked "weren't you lonely?": "No. 'Far from feeling lonely or abandoned, I feel very much a part of what is taking
  place on the lunar surface.'" (Collins, Carrying the Fire, via Universe Today:
  https://www.universetoday.com/34905/q-a-with-apollo-11-astronaut-michael-collins/). He did admit "a feeling of
  solitude" (HuffPost), so the script says only that he said he wasn't lonely.
