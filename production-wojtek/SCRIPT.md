## Facts (with sources)
- A Syrian brown bear cub bought at a railway station in Hamadan, Iran, in 1942 by soldiers of the Polish II Corps,
  evacuated from the Soviet Union. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wojtek_(bear) ; Stars and Stripes:
  https://europe.stripes.com/military-life/military-history/wojtek-the-polish-solider-bear.html
- He grew up with the 22nd Artillery Supply Company and mimicked the soldiers lifting crates (Wikipedia; Vis Lardica:
  https://www.vislardica.com/blog/2021/6/4/the-wojtek-memorial-edinburgh).
- To get him onto a British transport ship to Italy he was officially enlisted as a private, with a service number,
  pay book and ration card (Wikipedia mirror; Stars and Stripes).
- At the Battle of Monte Cassino (1944) he helped carry ammunition crates (Wikipedia; Britannica:
  https://www.britannica.com/print/article/2226582).
- Promoted to corporal (Britannica). Later lived at Edinburgh Zoo, visited by his old comrades; died 1963.
