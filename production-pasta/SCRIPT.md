## Script (148 words)
1. One summer night in Rome, neighbours heard crying, and called the police.
2. Four officers rushed over, ready for anything.
3. No burglar. No scam. No crime at all.
4. Just Michele, ninety-four, and his wife, Jole. Married almost seventy years.
5. They had been watching the evening news. So much bad news, it made them cry.
6. It was August. The neighbours were away. Nobody had visited in a long time.
7. On the table: a few grapes.
8. So one officer cooked them spaghetti. Butter. Parmesan.
9. The other three pulled up chairs, and just talked with them.
10. The police wrote: there was no one to save. Only two lonely souls to reassure.
11. That summer, their slogan was: we're always there.
12. Not everyone has four officers and a pot of pasta.
13. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny is always there, to listen, to talk, day or night.
14. Search Feeloy on the App Store.
