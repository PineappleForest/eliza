## Facts (with sources)
- Avon and Somerset Police launched the Chat Bench in Burnham-on-Sea (Somerset) in spring/June 2019, timed to UN World
  Elder Abuse Awareness Day; the first on the seafront Esplanade, a second in Taunton.
  - https://burnham-on-sea.com/?p=129837 ; https://www.goodnewsnetwork.org/english-police-create-chat-benches/
- The sign: "The 'Happy to Chat' Bench: Sit Here If You Don't Mind Someone Stopping To Say Hello." (yellow sign, ~100 m
  from the pier). https://www.relevantmagazine.com/current/this-town-created-chat-benches-to-fight-loneliness-in-their-community/
  ; https://uk.news.yahoo.com/happy-chat-bench-really-tackle-151945049.html
- It is just a sign on an existing bench (PCSO Tracey Grobbeler; Marple website: "simply involves adding a laminated sign").
- Spread: "It started in Burnham-on-Sea last year and has since spread across Europe and to Australia and North America."
  (BBC via newssniffer: https://newssniffer.co.uk/articles/1880216/diff/0/1)
- Sgt Ashley Jones, who introduced it, received an MBE (not used in the script).
