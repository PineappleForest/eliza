## Facts (with sources)
- Designed for 90 days; landed 24 Jan 2004 on Meridiani Planum; lasted 15 years and 45 km.
  Astronomy Now: https://astronomynow.com/?p=28761 ; Adler Planetarium: https://www.adlerplanetarium.org/?p=294263
- Contact lost in June 2018 during a planet-wide dust storm that blocked sunlight from its solar panels (AP via KNX:
  https://www.audacy.com/knxnews/blogs/nasas-opportunity-rover-dies-15-years-after-landing-mars).
- "My battery is low and it's getting dark": NOT literal words. Science reporter Jacob Margolis said NASA scientists
  told him the last data "effectively translated" to that (ABC7: https://abc7news.com/amp/post/my-battery-is-low-and-its-getting-dark-opportunitys-last-message-to-scientists/5137733/).
  Script says "loosely translated".
- More than 1,000 recovery commands, all in vain (AP via CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2019/02/13/nasa-rover-opportunity-finally-dies-on-mars-after-15-years.html).
- 12 Feb 2019: final attempt, with one last wake-up song, Billie Holiday's "I'll Be Seeing You"; declared over
  13 Feb 2019, eight months after contact was lost (Astronomy Now; AP).
