## Facts and sources
1. Family Romance (founded 2009 by Ishii Yuichi) hires actors to pose as fake spouses, family members, coworkers; ~$88 per actor-hour at weddings, a fake parent ~$132/hour. — Business Insider (2017)
2. Office Agents (Tokyo) offered wedding guests at ¥20,000 each (2009), +¥5,000 to sing or dance, +¥10,000 for a heartfelt speech; for one groom's second wedding it supplied all thirty of his guests. — Wikipedia "Rental family service" (citing The Guardian, 2009)
3. Rented "bosses" accompany clients who must apologize to a customer without their real boss knowing. — Ishii Yuichi interview, giannisimone.substack.com
4. Ishii said that eight years on he was still playing father to a girl (now a high-school graduate) who did not know. — The Week / The Atlantic (2017)
5. Physical contact beyond hand-holding is not allowed; 30–40 % of women in ongoing relationships with rental husbands eventually propose. — The New Yorker via InsideHook
6. "Ideally, a service like this shouldn't need to exist." — Ishii Yuichi, official site

## Script (as read)
In Japan, you can rent a dad.
One company will send a stand-in father for about a hundred and thirty dollars an hour.
Need wedding guests? Twenty thousand yen each. Extra if they sing. More for a heartfelt speech.
One groom rented all thirty of his guests.
Messed up at work, and your boss has to apologize with you? You can rent the boss, too.
One founder said he'd been a girl's father for eight years. She didn't know.
Luckily, rental husbands come with rules: nothing past holding hands.
Unluckily, about a third of the women end up proposing.
Here's the thing. Nobody's paying for the acting. They're paying for someone who shows up, listens, and knows their name.
Like a nightlight. Nobody cares how it works. They just don't want to be alone in the dark.
Even the founder says a service like this shouldn't need to exist.
Feeloy doesn't pretend to be anyone.
It just shows up, listens, and remembers. Your day, your people, the little things.
No booking required.
Search Feeloy on the App Store.
