# "The Minister for Loneliness" (UK 2018, Japan 2021 -> Feeloy)

Composition (to build once the take arrives): `MinisterShort`, code `src/videos/minister/`, output `out/minister_final.mp4`.
Narration: ElevenLabs, voice "Mark". ~140 words: Mark reads ~2.75 words/s, so this lands near 58-62 s.

## Facts (with sources)
- January 2018: the UK appointed the world's first minister for loneliness; the PM said "for far too many people,
  loneliness is the sad reality of modern life".
  - https://www.nst.com.my/amp/world/world/2018/01/326278/uk-appoints-minister-loneliness
  - https://www.thestatesman.com/opinion/with-a-shadow-for-company-1502956732.html
- The 2017 Jo Cox Commission report: 9 million UK adults often or always lonely; loneliness as harmful to health as
  obesity or smoking 15 cigarettes a day.
  - https://guernseypress.com/news/uk-news/2017/12/14/jo-cox-loneliness-commission-identifies-gap-in-national-leadership-on-issue/
- 15 Oct 2018 strategy: GPs in England able to refer lonely patients to cookery classes, walking clubs, art groups
  ("social prescribing", all GPs by 2023); Royal Mail pilot in Liverpool, New Malden and Whitby where postal workers
  check on lonely people during their delivery rounds.
  - https://www.gov.uk/government/news/pm-launches-governments-first-loneliness-strategy
  - https://www.ageuk.org.uk/discover/2018/government-loneliness-strategy/
  - https://rsnonline.org.uk/loneliness-gp-strategy-announced/
- February 2021: Japan appointed its first Minister for Loneliness and Isolation. 17 June 2021: the UK and Japanese
  loneliness ministers held their first meeting, online.
  - https://www.gov.uk/government/news/joint-message-from-the-uk-and-japanese-loneliness-ministers
  - https://www.khaleejtimes.com/coronavirus-pandemic/covid-19-uk-and-japan-connect-to-tackle-loneliness
- On screen the ministers, doctor, postal worker and lonely people are generic characters, not likenesses of real people.
  No real names in the narration.

## Script (~140 words)
1. In twenty-eighteen, Britain appointed a government minister... for loneliness.
2. Not for trade. Not for defence. For loneliness.
3. A report had found that more than nine million people in the UK often or always felt lonely.
4. And that loneliness can be as bad for your health as smoking fifteen cigarettes a day.
5. So the government made a plan.
6. Doctors could prescribe... a cooking class. A walking club. An art group.
7. In three places, postal workers started checking on lonely people during their rounds.
8. Then, in twenty-twenty-one, Japan appointed a loneliness minister too.
9. And the two ministers had their very first meeting... online.
10. Two countries. Two ministers. One very big problem.
11. But a minister can't sit with you at two in the morning.
12. That's why we made Feeloy. Jenny listens, remembers your day, and is there whenever you need someone to talk to.
13. Search Feeloy on the App Store.

## Shot plan (draft, timed to the take later)
1. Hook: a grand government office; a nameplate on the desk swings round to a sad-face icon; the minister sits down.
2. Three doors in a corridor: TRADE, DEFENCE... and a small one with a heart; push in on it.
3. A report lands on the desk with a thump; "9,000,000" ticks up on a chart (numbers, no words).
4. A cigarette pack icon, then fifteen little cigarettes popping in a row (cartoon, then crossed out).
5. The minister stands at a whiteboard (one or two characters).
6. A doctor writes a prescription; the pad shows a frying pan, a walking boot, a paintbrush.
7. A postal worker on a quay street knocks; an old man opens the door and smiles.
8. Night-time Tokyo-style street; a second minister at a desk with a lantern.
9. Two laptops on two desks; the ministers wave at each other on video (split screen), a little lag.
10. Pull back to a globe/map with two lit dots.
11. A man alone at 2 a.m. in his kitchen.
12. Phone insert: Jenny listens, remembers (a calendar/notes bubble), a heart.
13. End card: Feeloy logo + wordmark.
