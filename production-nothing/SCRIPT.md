## Facts (with sources)
- Morimoto was fired from his office job in 2018; his superior criticised him for lacking initiative and "not doing
  anything" of value. He then began renting himself out as a person who does nothing.
  - CNBC (via NBC Connecticut): https://nbcconnecticut.com/news/business/money-report/this-japanese-man-earned-80000-a-year-from-doing-nothing/3467853
  - (Other outlets say he left a publishing job; we follow CNBC's "fired" wording.)
- Started June 2018 with a tweet: "I offer myself for rent, as a person who does nothing."
  - https://www.sunnyskyz.com/blog/3227/A-Japanese-Man-Rents-Himself-Out-To-Do-Nothing-He-Has-Thousands-Of-Clients
- Thousands of requests: ~3,000 by early 2021 (VICE); ~1,000 a year now (CNBC).
  - https://www.vice.com/en/article/v7mp8m/japan-unique-jobs-rent-person-do-nothing-shoji-morimoto
- Examples (CNBC): waiting at a marathon finish line; riding the Yamanote Line for 17 hours (13 laps); a farewell at a
  railway station; sitting in a cafe within sight of a woman while she served divorce papers.
- He keeps conversation minimal and doesn't act as a therapist (CNBC); "I think it comes down to needing 'the presence
  of one human being' in a given situation" (VICE).
